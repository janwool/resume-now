# ResumeNowOnline Social Launch Playbook

## Goal

Turn useful resume content into qualified visits to the free editor. The first 30 days prioritize trust, saves, outbound clicks, and completed sign-ups—not follower count alone.

## Core message

**Build, edit, and preview a complete resume for free.**

Lead with the free product experience. Mention the $5 purchase only when a user reaches export or explicitly asks about downloads: one purchase includes three downloads.

## Content rules

1. Use real templates and real screen recordings from the editor.
2. Make one clear claim per post and prove it on screen.
3. Never claim AI writing, ATS scoring, free downloads, review scores, customer counts, or guaranteed hiring results.
4. Answer comments before linking. A useful reply is more valuable than a promotional reply.
5. Add alt text to every static asset and captions to every video.
6. Log results 48–72 hours after publishing in the KPI Tracker.

## Weekly operating rhythm

- **Monday:** review previous results; choose two winning hooks.
- **Tuesday:** batch four Pinterest Pins and one carousel.
- **Wednesday:** record three vertical editor/tutorial videos.
- **Thursday:** write LinkedIn and X posts; schedule the next seven days.
- **Daily:** spend 20 minutes replying to genuine resume and job-search questions.
- **Sunday:** move published URLs and metrics into the operations workbook.

## Community reply patterns

- **“Is it really free?”** — You can choose a template, edit the complete resume, and preview it for free. A finished export is $5 and includes three downloads.
- **“Is this ATS-friendly?”** — The template uses readable sections and selectable text, but no service can honestly guarantee how every employer's system will score a resume. Tailor the content and follow the requested file format.
- **“Can I edit the whole template?”** — Yes. You edit the actual template and can review every page, rather than filling a separate form.
- **“Can I upload a photo?”** — Templates with an image element support photo upload and positioning inside the editor.
- **“Which template should I use?”** — Share the target role and experience level; we will suggest a suitable format.

## Measurement decisions

- Repeat a topic when it earns qualified clicks, saves, or substantive comments twice.
- Rewrite a weak hook before abandoning a topic.
- Stop a format after three comparable tests with poor retention and no qualified clicks.
- Do not boost a post until it has demonstrated organic intent.
