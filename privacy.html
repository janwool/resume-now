Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

In plain English

We collect the minimum information needed to run optional accounts and deliver the site. Your résumé text and uploaded photo currently stay in your browser.

Privacy questions →

1. Who we are

ResumeNowOnline provides free browser-based résumé templates, editing tools, and PDF export. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@resume-now.online.

2. Information we collect

Account information

If you create an optional account, we collect your name, email address and a securely hashed version of your password. We also create session records so you can remain signed in.

Historical order information

We may retain records for purchases completed before free PDF downloads were introduced, including order references, purchase email, status, and related support information. We do not receive or store complete card numbers.

Résumé content

Your résumé text, design changes and uploaded photo currently remain in local storage in your browser. They are not uploaded to our D1 account database. Clearing browser storage, using private browsing or changing devices may remove or make this local content unavailable.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may process IP addresses, request logs and security data needed to deliver and protect the service.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure optional accounts, maintain sessions, deliver the website, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, respond to support requests, and meet legal recordkeeping obligations.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use an essential session cookie to keep you signed in. The editor uses browser local storage to save résumé edits and related preferences on your device. We do not currently use advertising cookies or behavioral advertising trackers.

5. Service providers

Cloudflare provides hosting, security and D1 database infrastructure. A historical payment provider may continue processing support or refund matters for transactions completed before paid downloads were removed. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services and meet legal obligations.

6. Retention

Account information is kept while your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterward where required for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations. Active login sessions expire after 30 days. Historical transaction records may be retained as required for accounting, fraud prevention and legal compliance. Local résumé content remains in your browser until you clear it.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information, or to receive a portable copy. You can also clear local résumé data using your browser controls. Email us to make a request; we may need to verify your identity.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Our providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

9. Children

The service is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required by law.
