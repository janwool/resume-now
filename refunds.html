1. Overview

The Resume Export Pack is a digital product that provides three PDF download credits for a one-time $5 USD payment. We want the purchase terms to be clear and will review eligible refund requests fairly.

2. Unused purchases

If you contact us within 14 days of purchase and none of the three download credits has been used, you may request a full refund. We will generally approve eligible unused-purchase requests after verifying the order.

3. Purchases with used credits

Once a PDF export has been initiated, part of the digital product has been delivered and consumed. Refunds are therefore normally unavailable after any credit has been used. We will still review requests involving a verified technical failure that prevented delivery, an accidental duplicate charge or an unauthorized transaction.

4. How to request a refund

Email support@resume-now.online with the subject “Refund Request” and include:

  • The email address used for the purchase and your ResumeNowOnline account
  • The Creem order number shown on your receipt
  • The date of purchase and a short explanation of the issue

Do not email card numbers, passwords or identity documents.

5. Processing

Creem is the merchant of record and processes approved refunds to the original payment method. Bank and payment-method processing times vary. When a refund is confirmed, unused download credits associated with that purchase may be removed from your account.

6. Consumer rights

This policy does not limit any mandatory refund, cancellation or withdrawal rights available under applicable consumer law. For immediately delivered digital content, legal withdrawal rights may change once delivery or download begins, depending on your location and the consent collected during checkout.

7. Payment disputes

Please contact us first so we can investigate and resolve the problem. Creem may also issue or manage refunds and chargebacks under its buyer terms and merchant-of-record obligations.

8. Contact

For refund or delivery support, email support@resume-now.online. For the product description and current price, see the Resume Export Pack page.
